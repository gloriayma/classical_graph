**Sonata No. 1 in G minor (Szigeti):** "Much double-, treble- and quadruple-stopping, often with one of the constituent notes to be accented; regular bursts of pseudo-Bach, often in chromatic vein" with "tremolo sul ponticello" passages and a "Fugato" featuring "sextuple-stopping."
