**Bow Management**: For the continuous sixteenth notes, the right hand requires sustained engagement. Bow should remain flat on the hair and play straight to maintain clarity during string-crossing passages.

**Left Hand Efficiency**: Block fingerings are recommended—for example, using one finger to hold consecutive notes across strings. This reduces unnecessary motion.

**Intonation Strategy**: Keep the sharps bright throughout, playing very slightly high to lift tone and maintain cheerfulness throughout all movements.
