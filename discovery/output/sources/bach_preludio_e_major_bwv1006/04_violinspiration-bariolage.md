"One of the most famous examples of bariolage is without a doubt Bach's Preludio from his Partita No. 3."

Bach uses string crossings between adjacent strings and adds a third string at measure 17. The technique involves:
- Separate bows to ensure open string resonance
- Using different strings for the same note (open E and stopped E)
- Alternating wrist and arm motions for even string crossing
- Lower strings being sometimes more difficult to access

Broader technique content:
- **Left-hand approach**: Fingers don't need to lift; practice moving notes separately
- **Wrist-led motion**: Making small circles, rocking between adjacent strings
- **Arm motion**: Best for reaching higher strings and slurred passages
