Brahms wrote the Violin Concerto in the summer and early fall of 1878 for Joseph Joachim, who premiered it on January 1, 1879, with the composer conducting the Gewandhaus Orchestra.

The violinist played an active role in revising the solo part. Joachim playing over passages and making suggestions, sometimes rewriting sections of the solo part to make them more agreeable under the hand, more violinistic.

**Finale:** "All of Brahms's concertos end in his Hungarian/Gypsy mode." (Referring to the third movement.)
