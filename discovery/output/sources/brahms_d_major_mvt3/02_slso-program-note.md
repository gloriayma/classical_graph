The finale (_Allegro giocoso, ma non troppo vivace_) has "Hungarian flavor" that represents "a bow to Joachim, who not only was of Hungarian background but had himself written a 'Hungarian Concerto' and dedicated it to Brahms."

"The recurring principal theme of this rondo-form movement...evokes the distinctive violin style that Joachim knew and Brahms loved so well, but the intervening episodes are equally notable for their energy and bravura passagework."
