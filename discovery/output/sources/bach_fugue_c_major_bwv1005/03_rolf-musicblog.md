## Fugue (II. Fuga: Allabreve)

**Complexity & Length:**
"With its 354 bars, this is the longest and most complex of the fugues in the _Sei Solo_—it is technically extremely demanding, with polyphony full of (arpeggiated) 3- and 4-stop chords."

**Notation vs. Reality:**
"Many of the passages are impossible to play exactly, note by note... It would take an organ to realize the notation exactly as written."

## Largo (III. Largo)

**Tempo Consideration:**
The movement requires balancing slowness with forward momentum: "It can't be too slow, though, given the 4/4 (common time) signature."

**Phrasing Challenge:**
"The art here is to make the music 'breathe' without disrupting the flow."

## Allegro assai (IV. Allegro assai)

**Dual Challenge:**
The work demands both speed and clarity: "One challenge is maintaining sound and sonority at the fast pace."

**Structural Feature:**
"In Sonata No.3, only the final movement, _Allegro assai_, features two parts, each with repeat signs."
