Section 1 (Moderato): An imposing, virtuosic introduction with slow majestic energy by the orchestra, then a softer violin solo in common time.

Section 2 (Lento): The violin plays in lugubrious lento. This section has an improvisational quality; the melody is punctuated with difficult runs and other technically demanding figures, including flying spiccato and ricochet bowings.

Section 3 (Un poco più lento): The muted soloist plays a melancholic melody with the so-called reverse-applied dotted note rhythm featuring the Mannheim sigh style in 2/4 time.

Section 4 (Allegro molto vivace): The piece becomes extremely rapid. The challenging solo part consists mainly of long spiccato runs, along with double stops, artificial harmonics and left-hand pizzicato in 2/4 time.
