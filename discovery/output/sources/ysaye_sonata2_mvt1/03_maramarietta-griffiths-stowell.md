## Paul Griffiths

**Sonata No. 2 (A minor, originally fourth):** Titled "Obsession," this sonata directly engages with Bach's E major partita prelude, whose phrases are "answered, diverted, echoed and developed in an A minor context." The Dies irae plainchant melody permeates all four movements. The three internal movements are titled "Malinconia" (E minor, muted), "Danse des Ombres" (G major), and "Les Furies." The "Danse" converts the plainsong into a pizzicato sarabande and then six variations—folksong, musette, two-part invention in minor—before restoring the bowed sarabande. Ysaÿe integrates his Bach quotations meaningfully rather than directing rage toward his predecessor.

## Robin Stowell

**No. 2:** The "Danse des ombres" opens "with some formidable quadruple stopping in pizzicato"; other movements treat the Dies Irae plainchant.
