The _Recitativo and Scherzo_ for solo violin, published in 1911 as Op 6, bears the dedication 'À Eugène Ysaÿe, le maître et l'ami'. The Recitativo demands the parlando bowing that was a persuasive part of Kreisler's armoury, while the Scherzo calls for considerable virtuosity. A dozen years later Ysaÿe returned the compliment by dedicating the fourth of his solo sonatas to Kreisler.
