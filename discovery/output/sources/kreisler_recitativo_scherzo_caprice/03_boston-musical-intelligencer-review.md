Mondays at Jordan Hall began as the remarkable Ninomiya gave lovely accounts of four Fritz Kreisler miniatures. The Recitativo and Scherzo Caprice, Opus 6, is one of Kreisler's works with an opus number and perhaps the only one for solo violin. He dedicated it to his good friend, the great Belgian violinist, Eugene Ysaye, and is probably a return gift for Ysaye's dedication to Kreisler of the Belgian's Sonata No. 4 for solo violin. Ninomiya made the most of the Recitativo's drama with its colorful harmonies, chromatic elaborations and trills. And she dispatched the Scherzo-Caprice with virtuosic flair and feats of velocity, ending it all lightly and delicately. It was music by a virtuoso in Vienna for a virtuoso in Brussels, played now in Boston's Jordan Hall by a virtuoso. With Matti Fang-Bentov at the piano, Ninomiya went on to perform the Scherzo in the Style of Dittersdorf, Schön Rosmarin and the Liebesfreud. She dispatched all three works with the expansive tempos, continuous vibrato, expressive phrasing and melodic approach to passagework that characterize Kreisler's own playing.
