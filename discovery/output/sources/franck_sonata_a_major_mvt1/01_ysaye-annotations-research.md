Ex. 1 (mvt 1, bars 1-31):
"quaver = 144" tempo annotation aimed to create a serene, dreamy character suited to the initial wedding setting.

Ex. 2 (mvt 2, bars 57-59):
"Ysaÿe's fingerings are marked in red, with question marks next to my best guesses for the erased numbers"

Ex. 3 (mvt 2, bars 38-43):
"Ysaÿe annotates the first A in this bar with the second finger on the D string and the second A of this bar with the fourth finger on the G string" creating a bariolage portamento effect.

Ex. 4 (mvt 2, bars 52-59):
"Ysaÿe draws a line to explicitly indicate a portamento" between C sharp and G in bar 55.

Ex. 5 (mvt 2, bars 67-79):
"Ysaÿe annotates the second finger on F and the third finger on E flat, which seems to be an invitation to use a portamento with the 'preceding' finger."

Ex. 6 (mvt 2, bars 94-97):
"Ysaÿe annotates a down-bow on the first quaver" and "the second finger on 3 notes in a row (G sharp, B and A)."
