"The 19th century was a heyday for arrangements of all kinds. So it was natural that the composer and violin virtuoso Sarasate should turn to this genre to expand his repertoire. He composed numerous fantasies on successful operas of the time, but pride of place undoubtedly belongs to his Carmen Fantasy."

The edition highlights that Sarasate deliberately selected numbers "that most clearly convey the Spanish atmosphere of the opera," and created both a piano and orchestral accompaniment version. This represents "the first-ever critical edition of the work, and takes the sources for both versions into account."

The solo violin part includes annotations by Augustin Hadelich providing fingering and bowing guidance. The work is classified as difficulty level 8 (difficult).
