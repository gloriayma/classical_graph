The blog post discusses Henri Vieuxtemps' Violin Concerto No. 5 in A minor, op. 37, completed in 1859. The author notes it is "the most popular of his six violin concertos."

The piece exemplifies "the mid-19th century violin concerto with its wealth of passion and expressivity." The opening features "the minor dramatic mood so much in favor for 19th century concertos."

Regarding other Vieuxtemps works: The post mentions that violinist Hilary Hahn released a recording pairing Vieuxtemps' 4th Concerto with Mozart's 5th. In the comments, the author later states: "I am leaning towards the 4th as well. Hilary Hahn is a powerful advocate!"

A commenter observes that "Vieuxtemps' 4th Violin Concerto is much better. It's far more melodic (as in having good melodies) and more exciting."
