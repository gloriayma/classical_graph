## Paul Griffiths

**Sonata No. 4 (E minor):** Ysaÿe explicitly follows the Bach partita model. The opening movement is an allemande that switches from quadruple to triple time after introduction. The second movement is an authentic sarabande in triple time with "a concealed ostinato: a descending scale fragment A-G-F-E recurring every bar." The finale is a perpetuum mobile with a gigue-like character, featuring "rapid alternations of sixths and tenths in passage-work." All three movements remain in E minor until the finale moves to major at its conclusion.

## Robin Stowell

**No. 4:** The Sarabande uses "different typefaces...to indicate the voice leading and in which an inner melodic voice plays in left-hand pizzicato"; the Presto finale employs 5/4 meter.
