The introduction is "marked Moderato" with the violinist performing "a rolling melody over a simple chordal accompaniment." The passage describes an "arch-shaped chromatic gesture" that eventually transitions the piece forward.

The Tarantella portion is marked "Allegro vivo" and characterized as "a wild and, in good hands, exhilarating 6/8 time ride up and down the violin fingerboard."

Sarasate "clearly intended it more as a flashy virtuoso display-piece than as an ingratiating charmer."
