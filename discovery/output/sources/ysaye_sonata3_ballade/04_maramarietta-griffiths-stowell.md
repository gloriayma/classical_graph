## Paul Griffiths

**Sonata No. 3 (D minor, single movement):** Titled "Ballade," this piece combines Chopin-like and Brahmsian qualities through virtuosity and narrative thrust. The main body chains extensions from an assertive theme in proud dotted rhythm, preceded by an introduction featuring double stops and a "recitativo" section with chromatic melody.

## Robin Stowell

**No. 3:** Features "rapid alternations of sixths and tenths in passage-work in the single-movement 'Ballade'".
