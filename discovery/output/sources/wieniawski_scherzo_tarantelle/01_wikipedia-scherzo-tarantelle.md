_**Scherzo Tarantelle**_ Op. 16 is a virtuosic piece for violin with piano accompaniment composed by Henryk Wieniawski, written in 1855. It was likely inspired by Frédéric Chopin's _Tarantelle_, or possibly Karol Lipiński's transcription of it. Wieniawski dedicated it to his teacher, Lambert Massart.

The piece is a rondo with the form ABACA and a coda, with the B section being a _maggiore_ in G major and the C section being a _cantabile_ in D major. A version with orchestral accompaniment also exists, which features an extra 20 measures of introduction before the violin entrance.
