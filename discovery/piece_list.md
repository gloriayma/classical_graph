# Violin Repertoire — Seed Piece List

**Purpose:** the corpus of pieces for (a) the discovery pipeline in `violin_technique_discovery.md` and (b) the eventual repertoire graph. One list serves both.

**Selection criterion:** maximize source density per edge. Pieces on this list are included because they have rich online discussion — masterclasses, threads, blog posts, dissertations. If any piece yields fewer than 3 usable sources during scraping, drop it and log why.

**⚡ = TwoSet Violin covers this a lot** — bonus signal from their videos and comments. Some entries are on the list *because* of TwoSet coverage even though they'd otherwise be borderline.

**Granularity:** movements, per project convention. Short single-movement works are one entry.

**How to review:** uncheck any piece you want to drop, or add ones I missed. The executor will use the checked entries as the corpus.

**Approximate count target:** ~250 movements. Currently: ~265 — err on the side of inclusion, trim during scraping.

## Notes for the executor

- Where a work has multiple movements, treat each movement as an independent piece with its own `piece_id`, per project convention.
- Etudes/studies are pieces too — they get technique-required edges AND technique-develops edges (an etude *develops* the technique it's built around).
- The Ševčík books, Wohlfahrt, and Locatelli caprices are legitimately less-discussed on the open web than the concert repertoire. Include them anyway for graph structure; accept lower per-edge source counts on these.
- Piece ID slugs: use `composer_shorttitle[_key][_mvtN]` — e.g. `bruch_concerto1_gminor_mvt1`, `bach_partita2_ciaccona`, `paganini_caprice_24`, `ysaye_sonata3_ballade`, `kreutzer_no2`.
